Honoring the Legacy of

Grady Pough, Sr.

A Legacy Built on Education

Born on July 16, 1920, in Waynesboro, Mississippi, Grady Pough, Sr. believed deeply in the power of knowledge and the importance of lifelong learning.

Despite receiving only a third-grade education, he understood that education could open doors and create opportunities for future generations. He remained committed to staying informed, reading the newspaper daily and encouraging those around him to seek wisdom and understanding.

Through his example, Grady instilled these values in his children, who have continued to pass down his passion for education to their own children and future generations.

Today, the Krewe of Grady Pough, Sr. proudly honors his memory by investing in deserving students through scholarships and community service, ensuring that his legacy of learning, family, and opportunity continues to inspire others.

About KOGP

The Krewe of Grady Pough, Sr. (KOGP) is a nonprofit organization based in Mobile, Alabama. Our mission is to empower students through scholarships and community support, continuing the legacy of Grady Pough, Sr.

Driven by the generosity of donors, sponsors, and fundraising events, KOGP continues to invest in the next generation — creating opportunities that leave a lasting impact year after year.
